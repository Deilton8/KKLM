About KKLM Enterprise LDA

A Mozambican company in procurement, engineering, consulting, occupational health and safety, and water treatment.

Request a proposal

A Mozambican company
of multidisciplinary solutions

KKLM Enterprise LDA develops, procures, delivers and supplies integrated solutions for a range of economic sectors, combining technical knowledge, strategic partnerships and access to national and international suppliers. We work in mining, energy, civil construction, industry, services, the corporate sector, government institutions and community organisations.

Mission

To promote knowledge and good practice in safety, hygiene and the working environment, raising the quality of services in the corporate and industrial sectors.

Vision

To be a national benchmark for organisational excellence, with innovative solutions that value professionals and society.

Values

Ethics, competence, partnership, customer focus, passion for quality, professionalism, sustainability and dynamism.

Lucílio Mondlane

Co-Founder & CEO

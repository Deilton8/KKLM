6-step filtration

Electret-nano composite filter (Aero Hybrid Filter™) with three complementary layers.

Multi-fibre layer

Removes pollen and coarse dust and extends the filter’s service life.

Electret fibre layer

Removes fine dust with low pressure and high efficiency.

Nanofibre layer

Blocks germs and viruses and prevents the loss of efficiency caused by moisture.
